BEM · JFM 2024

EHD Droplet Deformation & Taylor-Melcher Circulation

Axisymmetric boundary element simulation of an uncharged leaky dielectric droplet suspended in an immiscible fluid under a uniform DC electric field. Demonstrates transient dynamics starting from initial prolate perturbation ($D > 0$) settling into steady oblate equilibrium ($D \approx -0.0864$) accompanied by quadrupolar internal circulation vortices driven by interfacial free charge accumulation.

Droplet Dielectrophoresis & Field Code Validation

Rigorous mathematical verification of our boundary element electrostatic solver against exact analytical solutions for a droplet subjected to a non-uniform divergent electric field. Subplots show: (a) Applied field $\mathbf{E}_\infty$ & potential $\Phi_\infty$; (b) Field magnitude $|\mathbf{E}|/E_c$; (c) Normal electric field continuity $E_n$ matching exact theory; (d) Free surface charge density $q(\theta)$ across polar angles $\theta \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ]$.

BEM · Multi-Boundary

Capillary & EHD Liquid Bridges Between Cylinders

Multi-boundary axisymmetric BEM modeling of an electrohydrodynamic liquid bridge anchored between two cylindrical electrodes ($+V_0$ and $-V_0$). Dynamic simulation traces the fluid deformation flow speed $|u|$ coupled with the continuation bifurcation diagram ($B_e = 12.0, B = 0.5, d = 0.5, \theta_0 = 15^\circ$) mapping bridge height $H_b = z_1(0) - z_2(0)$ versus cross-sectional area $A$ up to turning-point limit ($A_{\text{max}} = 12.09$).

LBM · In Post-Processing

Lattice Boltzmann Multiphase Solvers (D2Q9/D3Q19)

Mesoscopic Shan-Chen multiphase simulations for density ratios up to $1000:1$ and high Maxwell stress divergence. Video datasets capturing vortex shedding past micro-pillars and electro-osmotic ionic shielding in charge-regulated nanochannels are currently in post-processing and will be integrated here shortly.
