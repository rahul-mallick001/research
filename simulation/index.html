Adding Your Simulation Animations

To showcase your personal research results, place your simulation MP4 recordings or animated GIFs into assets/media/:

  • Videos: Export MP4 files from ParaView or Tecplot and set <source src="../assets/media/your-file.mp4">.
  • Animated GIFs: Place GIFs into assets/media/ and embed via <img src="../assets/media/your-animation.gif">.
  • High-Res Plots: Save PNG/SVG charts from Python Matplotlib or MATLAB directly into assets/images/.
