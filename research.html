Research Portfolio & Solvers

My research focuses on analytical formulations, computational methods, and high-performance algorithms for interfacial multiphysics, microscale electrokinetics, boundary element solutions, and mesoscopic lattice hydrodynamics.

Electrokinetics & EDL

Nonlinear Electroosmotic Transport in Nanochannels

We model ion concentration polarization, electric double layer (EDL) capacitance, and electroosmotic flow (EOF) in charged nanochannels. Surface chemistry equilibria (protonation/deprotonation) are dynamically coupled with the non-linear Poisson-Boltzmann and Nernst-Planck systems to predict current-voltage characteristics and streaming currents.

Key Equations:
∇²ψ = -(ρ_e / ε) = (2 z e n_0 / ε) sinh(z e ψ / k_B T)
u_EOF = -(ε ζ / μ) E_x [1 - ψ(y)/ζ]

Boundary Element Method

Interfacial Droplet Deformation via High-Order BEM

We developed a customized boundary element method code in C++/Fortran with regularized Green's function kernels to solve creeping Stokes flow coupled with electrohydrodynamic Maxwell stress jumps across deformable fluid-fluid interfaces. Mesh reparameterization algorithms prevent node clustering at sharp droplet tips during high electric capillary number tip-streaming.

Boundary Integral Formulation:
c_ij u_j(x_0) + ∫_Γ u_j T_ijk n_k dΓ = ∫_Γ Δf_i G_ij dΓ + [ΔT_M]·n

Lattice Boltzmann Method

GPU-Accelerated Multiphase Lattice Boltzmann Solvers

Mesoscopic hydrodynamics on D2Q9 and D3Q19 lattices with Shan-Chen pseudopotential and phase-field formulations for high density-ratio multiphase systems. Coupled with external electrostatic Poisson solvers to model droplet coalescence, breakup, and contact line dynamics on patterned superhydrophobic substrates.

Lattice BGK Algorithm:
f_i(x + c_i Δt, t + Δt) - f_i(x,t) = - (1/τ)[f_i - f_i^{eq}] + Δt F_i

Electrohydrodynamics

Taylor-Melcher Leaky Dielectric Interfacial Dynamics

Investigating electrohydrodynamic droplet deformations governed by the competition between charge relaxation time ($t_e = \varepsilon/\sigma$) and hydrodynamic deformation time ($t_h = \mu a / \gamma$). Our work reveals non-linear quadrupolar recirculating flow structures and explains prolate-to-oblate transitions under AC and pulsed DC electric fields.

Interfacial Shear Stress:
[[ n · T^H ]] + [[ n · T^M ]] = γ κ n | τ_e = q_s E_t

Computational Toolchain & High-Performance Computing

In-House Solvers

Object-oriented C++ and modern Fortran codes for Boundary Element formulations, adaptive quadrature routines (Gauss-Legendre, Telles transformation for singular kernels), and GPU CUDA implementations of LBM streaming-collision steps.

HPC Clusters & Parallelization

Deploying simulations on IIT Kharagpur's High-Performance Computing (HPC) Supercomputing facilities utilizing OpenMP multi-threading and MPI domain decomposition for million-node lattice grids.

Post-Processing & Visualization

Automated pipeline using Python (NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib), ParaView Python scripting, and Tecplot for vector field streamlines, isosurface rendering, vorticity contour mapping, and high-framerate animation exports.
