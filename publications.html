Journal Article 2025

Nonlinear electroosmotic flow and ion transport in charge-regulated nanochannels

Rahul Mallick, S. Mukhopadhyay, and S. Chakraborty

Physics of Fluids, vol. 37, no. 4, pp. 042008, 2025.

DOI: 10.1063/5.0210452 PDF
View Abstract

We investigate nonlinear ion transport and electroosmotic slip dynamics in nanochannels with charge-regulated solid-liquid interfaces. Using a coupled Poisson-Nernst-Planck and Navier-Stokes formulation, we analyze the effect of finite ion size and surface site dissociation equilibrium on the screening layer capacitance and mass throughput under high applied electric fields.

Journal Article 2024

Electrohydrodynamic deformation and tip-streaming of leaky dielectric droplets: A boundary element formulation

Rahul Mallick, D. Bandopadhyay, and S. Mitra

Journal of Fluid Mechanics, vol. 988, p. A34, 2024.

DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2024.312 PDF
View Abstract

We formulate an axisymmetric boundary element method (BEM) to study the non-linear deformation and interfacial charge dynamics of a leaky dielectric droplet under uniform and pulsating electric fields. The singular boundary integrals are resolved using regularized Green's function identities, revealing the critical electric capillary numbers triggering tip-streaming emissions.

Journal Article 2024

Lattice Boltzmann modeling of coupled electro-hydrodynamic multiphase flows with high density ratios

Rahul Mallick, P. Roy, and S. Chakraborty

Physical Review E, vol. 109, no. 3, pp. 035304, 2024.

DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevE.109.035304 PDF
View Abstract

A diffuse interface lattice Boltzmann method (LBM) is developed for multiphase fluids experiencing high electrostatic forces and density ratios up to 1000:1. The Shan-Chen interparticle potential force is augmented with an electrostatic body force term derived from Maxwell stress divergence, with stability verified against classical analytical benchmarks.

Journal Article 2023

High-order boundary element schemes for singular Stokes kernels in electrokinetic droplet interactions

Rahul Mallick and K. Sengupta

Computers & Fluids, vol. 261, pp. 105912, 2023.

DOI: 10.1016/j.compfluid.2023.105912 PDF
View Abstract

We present accurate numerical evaluation schemes for weakly singular and nearly singular integrals arising in boundary element solutions of Stokes flow in confined electrokinetic geometries. High-order transformation algorithms guarantee logarithmic precision without excessive adaptive grid refinement.
