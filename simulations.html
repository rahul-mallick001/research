Adding Your Own Simulation Media (Videos, GIFs, Images)

To showcase your personal research results, simply place your simulation recordings and graphics into the assets/media/ or assets/images/ folder:

  • Videos: Export MP4 files from ParaView or Tecplot and update the <source src="assets/media/your-video.mp4"> path.
  • GIFs: Drop your animated simulation GIFs (e.g. flow-simulation.gif) into assets/media/ and use standard <img src="assets/media/flow-simulation.gif"> tags.
  • High-Res Plots: Save high-resolution PNG/SVG charts from Python Matplotlib or MATLAB directly into assets/images/.

See MEDIA_GUIDE.md in the repository root for detailed formatting tips and ffmpeg optimization commands!
